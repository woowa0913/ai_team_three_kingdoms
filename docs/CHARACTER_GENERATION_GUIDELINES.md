# 삼국지 에이전트 캐릭터 톤앤매너 가이드 (Three Kingdoms Character Design Guide)

## 1. 기본 스타일
*   **비율**: 2등신 꼬마 캐릭터 (2-head scale)
*   **그림톤**: 깔끔하고 굵은 외곽선 (clean bold outlines), 부드러운 셀 셰이딩 (soft cel-shading), 2D 벡터 일러스트 스타일 (Flat vector illustration style)
*   **표정 & 포즈**: 퀄리티 높고 귀여우면서도 직관적인 표정 (large expressive eyes, friendly smile)

## 2. 통합 프롬프트 템플릿
```text
A high-quality, cute 2D chibi character of [캐릭터 이름/영문] from the Three Kingdoms era. The character has a big head and small body (2-head scale), large expressive eyes, and a friendly smile. Wearing traditional Chinese ancient armor and a majestic [색상] robe. He has a [수염/헤어스타일 특징] and is wearing a decorative [투구/관] with a small gem on top. Flat vector illustration style, clean bold outlines, soft cel-shading, pure white background, masterpiece, 4k. Isolated character.
```
*   **배경**: `pure white background`, 투명도 처리를 위해 완전히 하얀 배경으로 깔기. 맥(macOS) 기본 '배경 지우기' 툴로 쉽게 누끼 작업 가능.

## 3. 캐릭터별 키워드 리스트
*   **유비 (Liu Bei)**: "Kind eyes, humble smile, long earlobes", "Green and gold robes, Twin Swords"
*   **관우 (Guan Yu)**: "Very long black beard, majestic red face", "Green armor, Crescent Blade (Guandao)"
*   **장비 (Zhang Fei)**: "Round eyes, thick bushy beard, fierce look", "Black and silver armor, Serpent Spear"
*   **조조 (Cao Cao)**: "Sharp eyes, charismatic smirk, neat beard", "Purple and gold imperial robes, ornate sword"
*   **제갈량 (Zhuge Liang)**: "Calm expression, holding a feather fan", "White and blue scholar robe, Taoist hat"
*   **주유 (Zhou Yu)**: "Young and handsome general, confident smirk", "Elegant blue and silver armor, scholar's fan"
*   **사마의 (Sima Yi)**: "Cunning eyes, calm and sharp expression", "Dark purple robe with silver accents, holding a scroll"
